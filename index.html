Reviews & How to Play

Drillers

In Drillers, players pilot mechs through an abandoned mine to excavate valuables left behind by a forgotten corporation. It’s a fast deck-builder for 1-4 players that plays in about 90 minutes. The game ends early if your gem overflow reaches your progress track, and you lose points for finishing deep in the mine. Reviewers praise its efficient design, though they say it shows its full hand quickly, which limits replayability.

-60°

Designed by Alexander Pfister and published by Nanox Games in 2026, -60° combines worker placement with deck and pool building. It’s set in an alternate 1971. You lead a research team in Antarctica, where blizzards and extreme cold limit your actions and punish reckless choices. It’s recommended for fans of engine-building euros like Great Western Trail.

Greenwood

Greenwood is a strategy game from Capstone Games, where the peninsula is advertised as a sanctuary for magical animals but is secretly run by druids. As Primal Spirits, you recruit allies, cast spells, strengthen your defenses and break the captive beasts out onto submarines. The game plays over three nights for 1-4 players and takes 40 to 160 minutes. One reviewer found it smooth and engaging but not entirely fresh.
